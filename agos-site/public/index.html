Questions

Is Agos a lender?

No. Agos connects your business with SEC-registered financing companies and prepares your application so it's complete before they see it. The lender makes the final decision.

What does Agos Verified mean?

It means we've checked your business and every document in your file is complete and up to date. We forward your requirements to lenders only after you're Agos Verified, and only with your approval. It isn't a loan approval: the lender still makes the final decision.

Does it cost anything?

No. Signing up and getting matched is free for businesses. Our partner lenders pay us when a loan is released.

What documents will I need later?

Usually your SEC or DTI registration, mayor's permit, BIR-stamped income tax returns, recent bank statements and a valid ID. We'll tell you exactly what's missing.

How do you handle my information?

We collect only what's on this form: your name, business details, mobile number and email. We use it to contact you and, only with your approval, to introduce you to partner lenders. We never sell it. To see, correct or delete your information, email us at helloagos.ph@gmail.com.
