01
The Bill of Lading.

The original BL, or a telex release from your shipper. Without it, the shipping line won’t hand over the delivery order.

Tip. Ask your shipper to release it as soon as the vessel sails.

2 / 7
02
Invoice and packing list.

They must match each other and the BL: the same names, quantities and weights. Mismatches slow customs down.

Tip. Send us copies early. We check them before the vessel arrives.

Philindo Logistics
3 / 7
03
Permits and clearances.

Some goods need them before release, such as FDA for medicines, food and cosmetics.

Tip. Not sure if yours does? Ask us before you place the order.

Philindo Logistics
4 / 7
04
Certificate of origin.

Goods made in Indonesia can qualify for lower ASEAN duty rates with an ATIGA Form D.

Tip. Ask your shipper to apply for it when the goods ship.

5 / 7
05
Funds for duties and charges.

Duties, taxes, port and shipping-line charges are paid before release. Late funds mean storage costs.

Tip. We send you the estimate before the vessel arrives.
